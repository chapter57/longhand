Longhand presents

A Quiet Day

A small game about a writing day. Make coffee, take Juniper out, look out the window, then sit down at the desk and write.

The desk opens Longhand, a real writing app. What you write is saved in this browser as you type, alongside your other Longhand pieces, and Export saves a copy as a Word document, PDF, Markdown or plain text.

Arrow keys or WASD to walk · E or Space to use things
Or click or tap where you want to go.
