← LonghandThe Lost Pages

Three books, all now in the public domain: Kafka's The Metamorphosis (1915), Joyce's Ulysses (1922) and Fitzgerald's The Great Gatsby (1925). Run with the arrow keys (or the buttons, on a phone), jump with the up arrow or space, and land on the critics to knock them out. Every day, a new writer opens in the Longhand Almanac.
