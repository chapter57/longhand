← LonghandMrs. Dalloway · the game

From Virginia Woolf's Mrs. Dalloway (1925, now in the public domain): one day in June 1923, from Westminster to Bond Street to Regent's Park, and the party that night. Run with the arrow keys (or the buttons, on a phone), jump with the up arrow or space, and hop on the pigeons. Walk up to people to talk. Every day, a new writer opens in the Longhand Almanac.
