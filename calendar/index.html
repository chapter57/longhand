A writer for every day

Every day of the grid has a name on it: James Baldwin, Jane Austen, Jorge Luis Borges, Toni Morrison, Mary Shelley, Langston Hughes and hundreds more, each on the day they were born, or marked † on the day they died. Bloomsday is there, and the day Moby-Dick came out in America.

The months belong to Edgar Allan Poe, Charles Dickens, Elizabeth Barrett Browning, William Shakespeare, Walt Whitman, Federico García Lorca, Franz Kafka, Herman Melville, Leo Tolstoy, Arthur Rimbaud, Mark Twain and Emily Dickinson.
