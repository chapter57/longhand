    Writethe app

    A word processor that gets out of the way.

    Longhand opens to a blank page and nothing else. The buttons fade while you type. Quotes curl, dashes lengthen, and your work is saved as you go. When a piece is ready, export it as a Word document, a PDF, Markdown or plain text.

    Your writing is saved on your computer and never sent to us or anyone else. There's no account to make, and once you install it, Longhand works without the internet.

    Open Longhand Free. For Chrome or Edge on a Mac or Windows computer.
