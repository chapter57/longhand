A quiet place to write, read, and talk about both.

Longhand is a free writing app, a small literary journal, and a book club. It's for people who care how a sentence sounds, and who'd like some company while they work on theirs.

Writethe app

A word processor that gets out of the way.

Longhand opens to a blank page and nothing else. The buttons fade while you type. Quotes curl, dashes lengthen, and your work is saved as you go, as Rich Text files you can open in Word, Pages or Scrivener.

It works without the internet once installed, and it never asks you to sign in. Nothing you write leaves your computer.

Open Longhand Free. For Chrome or Edge on a Mac or Windows computer.
